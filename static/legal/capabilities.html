What Strlix can and can’t do

Last updated 22 September 2026 · Strlix soft launch

This page is the product limit list for the soft launch. It is not a promise of a private device, an App Store listing, or a granted Android assistant role.

Can

  • Open a shared cloud Android in the browser, phone-first, without a card during the free first month.
  • Tap, swipe, and type while your session holds the controller.
  • Run in-phone help (Ask / Live) when the agent and the stream are up. The AI can be wrong.
  • Save an email on the waitlist, or claim a session later. Payment is not required for either.
  • Record a free-month entitlement (default about 30 days, amount zero) without calling Stripe.

Can’t — read this before a demo

  • Not your phone. Strlix does not control the handset in your pocket. It controls a cloud Android we host.
  • Not a private phone for 30 days. The free-month row is an entitlement. The live session expires in about one hour on a shared device. Other people can be scheduled onto that same Android. Catalog cards are not all booted phones.
  • Emulator latency is not physical-phone latency. The live pool is one emulator unless a physical device is attached and measured. Sub-100 ms control has not been certified on a physical phone. Do not quote an emulator timing as a handset result.
  • ROLE_ASSISTANT is not claimed. AssistantRoleHook does not request or hold Android’s assistant role. Strlix is not the system default assistant, does not declare VoiceInteractionService, and must not be described as one.
  • No on-device model. The on-device model hook reports disabled. It does not invent model output.
  • No illegal-use warranty. Strlix does not authorize unlawful use and does not warrant that a given use is legal. You are responsible for complying with the law.
  • No live billing. Stripe live charges are off. Razorpay is not taking Indian-rupee payments. The free-month grant does not call Stripe. Using the service from India does not require a card and does not mean the data stays only in India.
  • No iOS app. There is no App Store build.
  • No GPU fleet in this soft launch. Don’t expect a GPU worker.
  • Play Store, banking, and calls on the cloud image are best-effort and often blocked. Don’t put a real payment instrument on the emulator.

Abuse limits stay on

Anonymous session create, login, and waitlist are rate-limited. If Redis is configured and is down, those limits fail closed (HTTP 429) instead of opening the service. The free month does not disable them.
