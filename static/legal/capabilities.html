What Strlix can and can't do

Last updated 21 September 2026

Can

  • Open a cloud Android phone in the browser, phone-first, without a card during the free first month.
  • Let you tap, swipe, and type on that cloud phone.
  • Run in-phone help (Ask / Live) against the cloud device when the agent and stream are up.
  • Save an email on the waitlist or claim a session later. Payment is not required for either.
  • Record a free-month entitlement (default 30 days, amount zero) without calling Stripe.

Can't — read this before a demo

  • Not your phone. Strlix does not control the handset in your pocket. It controls a cloud Android we host.
  • Emulator ceiling. The live pool is one shared emulator unless a physical device is plugged in and measured. Emulator latency is not a sub-100 ms certificate. The market-api free-month row is an entitlement (about 30 days). The live session broker still expires around an hour and does not keep a private phone warm for each signup.
  • ROLE_ASSISTANT is a stub. AssistantRoleHook does not request or hold Android's assistant role. Strlix is not the system default assistant, does not declare VoiceInteractionService, and must not be described as one.
  • No on-device model yet. The on-device model hook reports disabled. It does not invent model output.
  • No live billing. Checkout, if you hit it, stays in Stripe test mode. The free-month grant does not call Stripe.
  • No iOS app. There is no App Store build. Signing secrets are not in this repo.
  • No GPU fleet. GPU quota is still a support ticket. Don't expect a GPU worker.
  • Play Store, banking, and calls on the cloud image are best-effort and often blocked. Don't put a real payment instrument on the emulator.

Abuse

Anonymous session create, login, and waitlist are rate-limited. A configured Redis that is down fails closed (429) instead of opening the floodgates. The free month does not disable those limits.
