## Mistake restated
Competitor renders a latency badge that never moves (constant "5 ms"), because it's a hardcoded marketing string, not measured RTT. A reviewer who watches for 30 s sees zero jitter — physically impossible over WAN — so every other perf claim (FPS, uptime, region) becomes suspect.

## Strlix solution (concrete UX + IA)
1. **Measure or say nothing.** One rule: the HUD only shows numbers derived from `RTCPeerConnection.getStats()` (`candidate-pair.currentRoundTripTime`, `inbound-rtp.jitter`, `framesPerSecond`, `packetsLost`). If stats are unavailable (no WebRTC, MJPEG fallback), the chip renders `RTT —` in grey with tooltip "not measured on this transport" — never a fake number.
2. **Jitter is the proof.** The chip in the phone status bar (top-right of the device frame, inside the bezel, not a floating chat widget) shows `42 ms` plus a 60-sample sparkline of the last 60 s. Motion is the trust signal; a flat line would be visibly flat.
3. **Tap the chip → Connection sheet** (bottom sheet over the phone, phone-first IA): p50 / p95 / max RTT over the session, jitter, FPS, dropped frames, codec, relay vs. direct (TURN/STUN), region + node id, and "last measured 1.2 s ago". Includes a **Re-test** button that runs 10 pings and reports the raw sample list.
4. **Show bad regions honestly.** Region picker lists live measured RTT per region from the visitor's own browser (quick `/probe` HEAD ping per region edge, 3 samples), sorted worst-to-best allowed. Colour bands: <60 green, 60–120 amber, >120 red with copy "playable for turn-based, not FPS".
5. **Degraded truth states**: `stale` (>5 s no stats) greys the chip; `estimating` for the first 3 s; `measuring failed` never becomes 0.
6. **AI inside the phone**: the on-device assistant surface can say "your RTT rose to 180 ms, switch to Singapore node?" — reading the same stats store, never a separate claim.

## Android 7+ / graceful degradation notes
- Chrome 60-era Android 7: `getStats()` legacy callback shape — `statsProbe` feature-detects promise API, falls back to DataChannel echo RTT (`performance.now()` round trip), else shows `—`.
- No sparkline SVG animation under 2 GB RAM / `prefers-reduced-motion`: render numeric only, poll at 0.5 Hz.
- MJPEG/canvas fallback transport: RTT from echo pings only; label "estimated (echo)".

## Acceptance criteria
- Over any 60 s session, recorded RTT samples show ≥3 distinct values; a constant series fails CI.
- With WebRTC stats unavailable, UI shows `—` / "not measured" — never a numeric latency.
- Stats older than 5 s flip the chip to `stale` within 1 s.
- Connection sheet exposes p50/p95/max, transport (direct/relay), node id, and a working Re-test showing raw samples.
- Region picker values come from the visitor's own probes, including red >120 ms regions being shown, not hidden.
