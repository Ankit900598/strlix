## Mistake restated
EN/global builds ship Stripe card + PayPal only, because the CN stack (Alipay/WeChat) was built first and adding UPI/e-wallets means new KYC, FX, payout and refund plumbing per country. In India and SEA, card penetration and card success rates are low: users hit a checkout that offers no UPI, GoPay/OVO/DANA, GCash, TrueMoney, or Touch 'n Go, and abandon. The paywall becomes a geo-filter, not a payment page.

## Strlix solution (concrete UX + IA)
1. **Region-resolved method rail, decided before the sheet opens.** `GET /api/pay/methods?cc=IN` returns an ordered list from a static rail table (IN: UPI intent → UPI QR → card → netbanking; ID: QRIS → GoPay/OVO → card; PH: GCash → Maya → card; TH: PromptPay → TrueMoney → card; else: card → PayPal). Country from IP + SIM/locale, with a visible "Paying from 🇮🇳 India · change" chip — never a silent lock.
2. **UPI intent as the first-class path, not a fallback.** On Android the primary button is "Pay ₹X with UPI" which fires `intent://pay?pa=...&am=...#Intent;scheme=upi;end`. We render installed-app chips (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) by probing the intent, and always show a **UPI QR + copyable VPA** for desktop or when no handler resolves. No dead end.
3. **Poll-based confirmation inside the phone shell.** After the app switch, the checkout sheet shows a "Waiting for your UPI app…" state polling `/api/pay/order/:id` every 2s for up to 5 min, with "I've paid" (forces a re-check) and "Cancel". Coming back from GPay must land on a resolved screen, not a stale form.
4. **Price shown in local currency with the settlement note.** "₹749 / mo · billed by Strlix India" — no USD-only pricing, no surprise FX. Currency comes from the same rail table.
5. **IA:** payments live in the phone shell as an overlay sheet over the stream (stream keeps running, muted), plus `/account/billing` for history, retries, and method change. Failed payment never kills the session — it drops to a 10-min grace trial banner.

## Android 7+ / graceful degradation notes
- UPI intent works on API 24+; wrap `startActivity` equivalents in try/catch — if `ActivityNotFoundException`/no handler, auto-swap to QR + VPA copy within the same sheet.
- No WebView `Payment Request API` dependency; plain forms + intent links only.
- QR rendered as inline SVG (no canvas/WASM), ~240px, high contrast for cheap panels.
- Poll via plain `fetch` + `setTimeout`, not SSE/WebSocket, so proxies and doze don't break it.
- If JS fails entirely, the sheet is a server-rendered page with QR, VPA, and a manual "Check status" button.

## Payment notes

## Acceptance criteria

- With zero UPI apps installed, the QR + copyable VPA path completes the same test order.
- The cloud phone stream on :8789 stays connected through the entire checkout, including app switch and return.
