Support

Strlix preview · rg-zevi-cloudphone only

Email support@strlix.app for access problems, waitlist removal, or a lease that did not start. Include the time and the email you used. Do not send passwords, card numbers, or session tokens.

Before you write

  • The first month is free. You should not be asked for a card. If a screen asks for one, stop and email support — live Stripe is off.
  • The phone on screen is a cloud device (today, an emulator unless a physical phone is attached). It is not your personal phone.
  • Read what Strlix can and can't do before reporting a missing assistant or a latency number.

Status

API health is GET /health on market-api. It reports pay_mode, whether the free month is active, and whether a card is required. It does not include secrets.
