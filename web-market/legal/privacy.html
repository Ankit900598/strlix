Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026 · Strlix preview

Strlix is a cloud phone. The preview is built to keep personal data small: an email if you choose to save one, a session, and the phone lease.

What we store

  • Waitlist. Email and the time you joined (created_at). An invite code, if you used one, is stored only as a hash.
  • Account. Email and display name after you claim a session. Anonymous preview sessions have no email until you save one.
  • Lease. Which cloud device you were granted, for how long, and that the amount was zero during the free month.
  • Payments. Not used for the free month. If a test checkout runs, we store a provider intent id and status. We do not store card numbers.
  • Rate limits. A short hash of the client address in Redis, not the raw address in audit logs.

What we don't do

  • No live card charge during the free month.
  • No sale of waitlist emails.
  • No requirement to sign in with Entra for the phone preview. Entra is optional and off by default.

How to delete

Signed-in sessions can request a soft-delete from the API (/v1/privacy/soft-delete). Waitlist emails are separate: email support@strlix.app and ask us to remove that address. Hard-delete of backups is a later job, not an instant wipe of every replica.

Where it runs

The service runs in Azure resource group rg-zevi-cloudphone. The database is Postgres. Sessions and rate limits use Redis. See Support for contact.
