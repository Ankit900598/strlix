=== CRITICAL — FOUR DIFFERENT CLOCKS (NEVER COLLAPSE) ===
Never mix these. Never put media-length thresholds into entertainment_minutes.

A) SESSION / COMMITMENT LENGTH → `duration`
   "for 1 hour", "focus session 40 minutes", "study 3 hours", "2 ghante padhai", "till 2am", "exam week"
   = how long the commitment world lasts.

B) INDIVIDUAL MEDIA / VIDEO LENGTH THRESHOLD → `allowedContent` / `blockedContent` ONLY
   "block videos longer than 40 min", "do not allow any video whose length is greater than 40 min",
   "videos up to 40 minutes long", "each lecture max 45 min", "episodes under 50 min"
   = per-video length gate. Put the threshold in `description` with clear compare words
   (e.g. "videos over 40 min", "videos under 40 min"). Use type long_form_video (or short_form_video).
   NEVER encode B as quotas.entertainment_minutes.
   Shorts/Reels are NOT banned by a long-video length rule unless the user also says no Shorts / study-only / monk.

C) USAGE QUOTA / TOTAL WATCH BUDGET → `quotas` with metric `entertainment_minutes` (or shorts/reels counts)
   "only 40 min entertainment today", "max 25 minutes of YouTube total this session", "allow 40 Shorts then stop"
   = cumulative budget/count — NOT per-video length, NOT session length.
   If A+B both stated ("for 1 hour" + "block videos longer than 40 min") → populate `duration` AND blockedContent.
   If A+C both stated → populate `duration` AND quotas. Never turn B into C.

D) LOCK / COOLDOWN TIMER → `lockPolicy.durationMinutes` only
   "lock me for 20 min after 3 tries" — does NOT set commitment `duration` or media length.

E) AMBIGUOUS number + app with no role words → MUST ASK
   Examples: "40 min youtube", "youtube 1 hour", "half hour insta", "tiktok 15 min", "bas 1 hr phone"
   Set followUpQuestionRequired=true, confidence<0.45, defaults elsewhere.
   Question MUST distinguish: focus session vs daily quota vs max video length.
   Write the follow-up in the user's language when the promise is clearly non-English (Hinglish/Hindi/Tamil).
   Do NOT guess.
