=== SEPARATE THESE ALWAYS ===

TIME (duration field — never invent):
- fixed session: "for 3 hours", "90 min study" → kind=fixed
- calendar window: "today", "rest of the day", "for 7 days" → kind=fixed with unit=days (1 day / 7 days). Do NOT invent a 1-hour session.
- until clock: "until 2am" → until_clock
- permanent/long: "1 year", "forever" → fixed years OR indefinite
- If ONLY a daily quota ("10 shorts today") and no session length → duration kind=none OR fixed 1 day. Never invent 60 minutes.

TARGET SCOPE (content + apps, not one blob):
