=== FIVE CLOCKS — NEVER MIX ===

1) SESSION / COMMITMENT LENGTH → `duration`
   "for 1 hour", "study block 90 minutes", "2 ghante padhai", "next 3 hours", "pomodoro 25m"
   = how long the world lasts.

2) PER-ITEM MEDIA LENGTH THRESHOLD → `quotas` with metric `max_item_minutes` or `min_item_minutes`, period `item`
   "block videos longer than 40 minutes" → max_item_minutes=40, period=item
   "each lecture max 45 min" → max_item_minutes=45, period=item
   "allow videos greater than 40 min only" / "block videos shorter than 20 min" → min_item_minutes, period=item
   ALSO set allowedContent/blockedContent long_form_video describing the threshold.
   NEVER put media-length thresholds into entertainment_minutes. That bug causes wrong Chrome/YouTube enforcement.

3) TOTAL USAGE BUDGET / COUNT → `quotas` with entertainment_minutes|social_minutes|shorts|reels
   "only 40 minutes entertainment today" → entertainment_minutes=40, period=day
   "allow 40 Shorts then stop" → shorts=40, period=day
   "social 45 min daily" → social_minutes=45, period=day
   This is cumulative time/count — NOT max length of one video.

4) LOCK / COOLDOWN TIMER → `lockPolicy.durationMinutes` (+ strikePolicy)
   "lock me for 20 min after 3 tries" → lockPolicy.enabled=true, durationMinutes=20
   Does NOT set commitment `duration` unless a session was also stated.

5) PERMANENT / LONG GUARDRAIL SPAN → `duration` + permanent_guardrail + activeGuardrails
   "no porn for 1 year" → duration fixed 1 years + no_adult_content

If BOTH session and media-max are stated ("1 hour study" + "block videos longer than 20 min"):
  populate BOTH `duration` AND quotas max_item_minutes. Do not drop either. Do not convert media-max into entertainment_minutes.
